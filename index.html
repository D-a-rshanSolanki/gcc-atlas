Trust principle

In a production version, a provider can pay for a clearly labelled enhanced profile—not for a higher quality score or organic ranking. Outcome claims require evidence such as client references, disclosed methodology or independently verifiable case studies.

How a production record should work

1
Capture
Store original URL, publisher, publication date, retrieval timestamp and excerpt.
2
Resolve
Match aliases, parent company, legal entity, city, facility and announcement type.
3
Validate
Separate announced, planned, launched and verified facts; route conflicts to a human review queue.
4
Serve
Expose citations, last verified date, confidence rationale and correction history in every workflow.

Prototype limitation

This is a local, curated demonstration. It does not scrape the web, refresh market data automatically or verify provider delivery performance. The live AI endpoint is optional and requires the operator's own API key.
